### Preserving the Golden Heritage of Moroccan Jewry and the Teachings of the Chida

![Bet Midrash HaChidah NOHO](/images/bet-midrash-chidah-header.png)

**Welcome to Bet Midrash HaChidah** a vibrant Moroccan Orthodox community dedicated to keeping the beautiful traditions, melodies, and halachic rulings of our ancestors alive, inspired by the profound wisdom and love for Torah of the Chida (Rav Chaim Yosef David Azulay)

Bet Midrash HaChidah takes its name from Rabbi Chaim Yosef David Azulai (1724–1806/1807), the renowned Sephardic Torah scholar known as the Chida. Born in Jerusalem, the Chida became celebrated for his halachic writings, his travels among Jewish communities, and _Shem HaGedolim_, his landmark work documenting Jewish scholars and their books

## About Our Synagogue

### A Living Legacy of Faith and Devotion

Our Kehillah is more than just a place to pray; it is a warm, welcoming home for everyone who walks through our doors. Grounded in the rich spiritual tapestry of Moroccan Orthodox Judaism, we strive to blend deep-seated traditions with a welcoming community atmosphere

### What Defines Us

• The Moroccan Nusach: We take immense pride in preserving our authentic liturgy, utilizing the unique, soul-stirring melodies (piyutim) and traditional cantillation passed down through generations of Moroccan rabbis
• In the Footsteps of the Chida: Named in honor of the Chida—the legendary 18th-century Sephardic posek, kabbalist, and emissary—we emphasize his core values: meticulous Torah scholarship, unconditional love for every Jew, and a deep respect for minhag
• Warm Hospitality (Hachnasat Orchim): True to our North African roots, our community is built on warmth, open doors, and bringing people together over uplifting Tefillah and festive community meals
